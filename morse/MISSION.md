# Mission: Morse Code

## Why
A personal challenge: to really own a skill that most people only know as "SOS", by learning to hear Morse as sound patterns rather than decoding dots and dashes from a chart.

## Success looks like
- Recognise all 26 letters and 10 digits **by ear alone**, with no chart, at real character speed (~20 WPM characters).
- Send every letter and digit cleanly in the browser by tapping a key, with timing a decoder reads correctly.
- Copy short random groups of characters at 20 WPM character speed with ≥90% accuracy.

## Constraints
- 10–15 minutes of practice on most days. Lessons and drills must fit inside one session.
- Starting from zero (knows SOS at most).
- Sending happens on a keyboard or tap key in the browser. No hardware.
- No deadline: favour steady spaced practice over cramming.

## Out of scope
- Ham radio licensing, on-air procedure, Q-codes and abbreviations (for now).
- Punctuation and prosigns beyond the basics, until the alphabet and digits are solid.
- Head-copying words and conversations: a possible later mission.
